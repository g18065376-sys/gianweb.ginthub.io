SOCIAL MEDIA & TEENAGERS.

Understanding the positive and negative effects of social media on teenagers.

01. Introduction

Social media has become an important part of teenagers' daily lives. Many teenagers use platforms like Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube to communicate, learn, and share ideas.

02. Positive Effects

  • Communication: Social media helps teenagers stay connected with friends and family.
  • Education: Teenagers can find useful information and learn new things.
  • Creativity: They can express their ideas through videos, photos, and artwork.

03. Negative Effects

  • Distraction: Spending too much time on social media can interrupt studying.
  • Sleep Problems: Using social media late at night can reduce sleep time.
  • Misinformation: Not everything shared online is true or reliable.

04. Our Opinion

In our opinion, social media is neither completely good nor completely bad. It depends on how we use it.

Teenagers should use social media wisely, responsibly, and in moderation. We should focus on the benefits and avoid its negative effects.

05. Conclusion

Social media can be a useful tool for teenagers if they know how to manage their time and use it responsibly.
